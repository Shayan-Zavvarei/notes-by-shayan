Absorption vs Exhaustion

Two order-flow signatures resolve what a candle alone cannot. Absorption is passive orders soaking up aggression without price moving; exhaustion is the aggressive side running out of fuel.

Absorption says someone is quietly filling here; exhaustion says the last of the crowd just got in, and there is no one left to push. Both pair with delta divergence and the CHoCH that follows — they upgrade or veto a setup the structure already proposed.

The Texture of the Reaction at a Level

Absorption at an order block means the institution is loading the position you want to join — wait for the displacement away from it. Exhaustion at a liquidity pool means the sweep is complete and the fuel is spent — the reversal is now the higher-probability path. Switch between the two.
