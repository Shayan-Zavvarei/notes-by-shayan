Liquidity Sweep + OB Reversal

Win ~48–55% (est.) R:R 1:4 TF 5m–15m / 1H London / NY Open XAUUSD · EURUSD · indices · BTC

Wait for the pool to be swept (spike, wick, close back inside), then a CHoCH, then enter the post-CHoCH OB. Stop beyond the sweep wick; target the opposite pool. Play the reversal.

BOS + Retracement to OB
