The News-Sweep Setup

The model assembled into a single, gated trade. You are not predicting the number — you are positioning for the algorithm to use the number.

No displacement, no CHoCH, no trade. The news sweep without confirmation is just gambling on a candle. Spreads balloon around releases — widen the stop and size down, or skip if the structural stop is untenable.

9.2

Pre-conditions → Sweep → CHoCH → Entry → Draw

Pre-conditions must exist before the release: a clear HTF bias and draw; an obvious liquidity pool resting on the side opposite the draw; a scheduled high-impact event in a valid killzone. Execution is strict — sweep against bias, wait for displacement (CHoCH + FVG), enter on the retrace after a candle close, stop beyond the spike, target the draw. Hover any element.

Hover the chart or a chip: pool → news sweep → CHoCH → entry → draw. No displacement / no CHoCH = no trade.
