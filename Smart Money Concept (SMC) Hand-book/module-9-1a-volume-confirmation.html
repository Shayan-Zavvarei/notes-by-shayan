Volume as Confirmation

Volume answers one question at a PD array: did size transact here, or did price just drift? Spikes on the sweep and the displacement confirm participation; a quiet pullback confirms there is no fresh supply.

On spot forex there is no central tape — your platform shows tick volume (price changes), a proxy, not truth. For a real read, pull volume from the correlated CME future (6E, GC). Volume confirms; it never leads.

9.1

Reading the Two Spikes & the Quiet Pullback

The two candles that should carry volume are the sweep (liquidity taken) and the displacement (the impulsive leg that breaks structure and leaves an FVG). The candle that should be thin is the retracement into the order block — a genuine pullback is a lack of supply, not a fresh wave of selling.
