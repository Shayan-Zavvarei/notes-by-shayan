Normal Drawdown vs Edge Decay

The hardest discipline is distinguishing a normal drawdown (variance the math guarantees) from genuine edge decay (the premise no longer holding). They feel identical — which is why feeling cannot be the judge.

A losing streak that stays within the maximum drawdown your backtest already showed is expected and is not evidence the edge is broken. Edge decay is performance that degrades beyond statistical expectation across a meaningful sample.

9.3

Let the Band, Not the Feeling, Decide

Three signals separate decay from noise: metrics breaching control limits (outside ~2σ over 30+ trades); the premise disappearing (a regime change); and — most diagnostic — clean A-grade execution with negative results, which points to selection, not execution.
