The Liquidity Sweep

How retail becomes fuel. Price spikes below obvious support to trigger resting sell orders, institutions absorb them, then price reverses — the hunter waits for confirmation.

Your stop loss is not protection — to an institution, it is an order. When your stop sits below an obvious low alongside thousands of others, that cluster is a pool of sell orders a large buyer can trigger and absorb.

How Retail Becomes Fuel

A stop hunt is mechanical, not conspiratorial. Long traders place stops below support; breakout traders place sell-stops below it. Both rest sell orders in the same zone. A large buyer pushes price down through the low, fills against those orders, then price snaps back. Press play to watch the sequence, or hover the candles to inspect each stage.
