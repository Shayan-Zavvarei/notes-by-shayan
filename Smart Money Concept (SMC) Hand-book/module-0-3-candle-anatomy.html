Candlestick Anatomy

Reading a clean chart means reading price, structure, and time — nothing else. Each part of a candle means something specific in the SMC context.

The close is the most important single price. A "break" of a level in SMC is usually defined by a body close beyond it, not a wick poke. Strip every indicator — you want the cause (order flow expressed as price), not the lagging echo.

Body, Wick & Close

Hover the wick or body of the bullish candle — or use the buttons — to inspect each part. The body is the conviction range; the wick is rejection; the close tells you who won the period. The bearish candle shows the mirror: open on top, close on the bottom.
