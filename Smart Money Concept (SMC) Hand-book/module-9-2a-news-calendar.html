The Calendar as a Timing Map

Retail trades the content of news ("good NFP, so buy"). SMC trades the timing and mechanics. A high-impact release is a scheduled liquidity event — a moment the algorithm has license to deliver price violently in both directions.

The calendar is a clock for when volatility will fire; the HTF draw still tells you where price is going. News is the catalyst, not the compass. Trading with the calendar means using its timing while ignoring its narrative.

9.2

Mark the Event, Plan the Window

Mark the high-impact events for your instruments and note the exact release time. Then make a pre-committed decision per event: it is your setup's catalyst (you have a plan), or you are flat through it. The expansion after the release is where you trade the reaction — never the spike. Toggle the do-not-touch window, or replay.
