The If-Then Rule

Discretion is where edges go to die. The cure is to encode every decision as an if-then statement written before the session, so that in the moment you execute a rule rather than form an opinion.

"I'll buy if it looks good" is not a rule. A complete rule names four things: the trigger, the action, the invalidation, and the management. You are not trying to out-discipline your emotions in real time — you are removing the decision from the moment entirely.
