One Frame, Two to Three Triggers

Do not trade every setup in the handbook. Choose two or three that are complementary — each covering a market condition the others do not.

Coherence comes from a shared top-down frame, not from the triggers being similar. Every setup is filtered through the same HTF bias, draw, and premium/discount logic — only the trigger differs. If none is present, there is no trade — and that is the system working, not failing.
