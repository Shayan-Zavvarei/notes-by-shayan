2.1

The Liquidity Concept — buy-side vs sell-side

Buy-side liquidity sits above highs (made of buy orders); sell-side sits below lows (made of sell orders). Price rallies into BSL so sellers can fill, and drops into SSL so buyers can fill. Hover each pool.

2.2

Liquidity Pools — four types

Equal highs/lows, trendline liquidity, pattern liquidity, and old highs/lows. The more obvious and famous a level, the more reliably it holds stops. Switch between the four pool types.

2.3

Sweep Mechanics — spike, wick, close back inside

A sweep is in-and-out: it pierces the level, leaves a wick, and closes back inside. A break is through-and-gone: it closes and holds beyond. Play the sweep, or compare it to a genuine break.

2.4

Inducement — the bait before the real zone

Inducement is engineered liquidity placed in front of the true point of interest. Early traders enter at it; their stops fuel the move into the real zone. Step through the sequence.

2.5

Premium & Discount — the dealing range
