1.1

Swing Points — strong vs weak, internal vs external

The 3-candle rule: a swing high is higher than the candle on either side; a swing low is lower than both neighbours. Strong points (which produced a break) are defended. Weak points are targets. Hover any marker.

1.2

Market Structure Phases

Bullish is a staircase of higher highs and higher lows. Bearish is lower highs and lower lows. Ranging is overlapping chop that sweeps both sides. Switch the regime to redraw.

1.3

Break of Structure — close, not wick

A BOS needs a body close beyond the prior swing. A wick that pierces and closes back is a liquidity sweep, not a break. Toggle between the valid break and the trap.

1.4

Change of Character — the first crack

BOS = continuation. CHoCH = the first opposite-direction break, signalling a possible reversal. The reliable version comes after a liquidity sweep. The trap version fails and resumes trend.

1.5

Multi-Timeframe Alignment

The higher timeframe dictates delivery; the lower timeframe times the trigger. The A+ trade is all three pointing the same way at the moment of entry. Watch the sequence play top-down.
