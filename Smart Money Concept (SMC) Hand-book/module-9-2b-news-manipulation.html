The First Move Is the Fake Move

News supplies the volatility that is the sweep. In the accumulation → manipulation → distribution arc, a release is the perfect cover for the manipulation leg: the spike "explains itself," so the stop-run looks like a fundamental reaction.

Nothing new is happening — it is the same model with a scheduled catalyst attached. When a high-impact release prints, do not chase the first candle. Watch which liquidity it takes; if it sweeps against your bias then displaces back, the fake move just handed you the discount.
