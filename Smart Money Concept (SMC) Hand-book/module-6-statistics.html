Expected Value Calculator

EV per trade = (Win% × Avg R:R) − (Loss% × 1), assuming a full 1R loss on losers. Move the sliders and watch the math, the weekly expectancy, and the verdict update live.

Break-even Win Rate by R:R

The win rate at which a setup merely breaks even is 1 / (1 + R:R). Above the line you profit; below it you lose — regardless of how good the entries "feel". The bar shows how higher R:R slashes the win rate you need.
