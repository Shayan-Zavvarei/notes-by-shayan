Delta Divergence

Delta is aggressive buying minus aggressive selling; cumulative delta (CVD) is the running total. It exposes who is being aggressive underneath a move — and when a new extreme is made on less force than the last.

Price prints a new low into a sweep, but delta prints a higher low: the low was made on less net selling. Sellers push harder for less result — the fingerprint of buyers absorbing. A confirmation, never a trigger. Use real futures tape, not spot ticks.
