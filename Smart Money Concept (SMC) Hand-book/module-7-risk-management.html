7.2

Break-even Win Rate by R:R

Break-even Win% = 1 / (1 + R:R). At 1:3 you need only 25% wins; at 1:1 you need over 50%. This is why high-R:R SMC setups stay profitable below a coin-flip win rate. Bars show the threshold; the line is your selected win rate.

The Circuit-Breaker Ladder
